# LexLearn — Qatar First-Year Sources of Obligations Curriculum Map

Date: 2026-10-06
Scope: publicly verifiable Bachelor of Law curricula in Qatar for the first-year course covering Sources of Obligations.

## 1. Qatar University — College of Law

### Placement
- Course: **LAWC 213 — Sources of Obligations**
- Credit hours: **3**
- Prerequisite: **LAWC 101 — Introduction to Law**
- Current catalog: Undergraduate Student Catalog 2025/2026.
- The course is an active compulsory law course and appears in Spring 2026 examination schedules.

Official sources:
- https://www.qu.edu.qa/en-us/Colleges/law/departments/private-law/Pages/course-description.aspx
- https://qataruniversity-prd.qu.edu.qa/ar/Colleges/law/departments/private-law/Pages/course-description.aspx
- https://qttsc.qu.edu.qa/en-us/students/documents/undergraduate-student-catalog-2025-2026-en.pdf

### Verified substantive coverage
The current official descriptions/catalog cover:

1. **General theory of obligation** — concept, characteristics and types.
2. **Contract** — concept/classifications; autonomy of will and limits; formation; consent; expression of will; subject matter; cause; defects of will; invalidity and consequences; effects; contractual liability; pacta sunt servanda; good-faith performance; unforeseen circumstances; termination.
3. **Unilateral will** — conditions/effects and promise of reward.
4. **Tort / wrongful act** — elements, personal liability, liability for others, and liability for damage caused by things.
5. **Beneficial act / unjust enrichment** — requirements/rules; management of another's affairs (فضالة); recovery of undue payment.
6. **Law / legislation as a direct source**.
7. **Learning modes** — theory, legal-text analysis, contemporary issues, hypothetical cases and practical application.

## 4. Proposed 6-week LexLearn Qatar core

The 30-session / 6-week structure is retained while coverage is broadened to match verified first-year content.

### Week 1 — Contract foundation and formation
- concept and classifications of contract; autonomy of will and limits; offer/acceptance; consent; Article 64 as a text anchor; decisive-fact spotting.

### Week 2 — Consent and validity
- expression of will; special forms; defects of will; subject matter; cause; validity/invalidity and consequences; legal precision.

### Week 3 — Contract effects and liability
- binding force; contractual effects; good faith; contractual liability; unforeseen circumstances; termination; boundary cases.

### Week 4 — Unilateral will and source classification
- unilateral will; promise of reward; distinguishing source types; law as direct source; delayed retrieval.

### Week 5 — Tort / wrongful act
- elements; personal liability; liability for others/things; issue spotting; causation/fact differentiation; short exam construction.

### Week 6 — Unjust enrichment + integration
- unjust enrichment; الفضالة; undue payment; direct-law source recap; mixed-source cases; cumulative retrieval; exam synthesis; personalized post-program plan.

## 5. Production content rules

Each item must carry institution/course ID, country, unit/concept, learning dimension, difficulty, source IDs/effective date, rubric, legal-review status, learning-review status, version and exam-frequency provenance (or unknown).

No item should be treated as university-approved merely because the subject appears in an official course description. Faculty/authorized academic review remains a separate approval gate.

## 6. Adaptive-analysis coverage

The learner model should separately estimate recall, understanding, legal precision, application/transfer, exam construction and delayed retention. It should also maintain uncertainty/evidence strength, trend, confidence-performance calibration, error type, repeated misconception memory, institution/course/content version. Training should explicitly use the strongest demonstrated skill to repair the weakest demonstrated skill wherever possible.
