# Qatar — First-Year Sources of Obligations Curriculum Map

Verified: 2026-10-06

## Purpose
This document defines the Qatar content boundary for LexLearn's first institutional pilot in **Sources of Obligations**. It separates official curriculum evidence from LexLearn's adaptive instructional design.

## 1. Qatar University — College of Law
**Course:** LAWC 213 — Sources of Obligations  
**Placement:** First Year, Spring (in the published study plan used for mapping)  
**Credits:** 3  
**Prerequisite:** LAWC 101

Official course-description coverage includes:
- Contract: elements/formation, conditions and defects, effects, contractual liability.
- Unilateral will and applications including promise of a reward.
- Tort / unlawful act: elements, personal responsibility, responsibility for acts of others, and responsibility for things.
- Unjustified enrichment and applications including undue payment and officious management.
- Legislation/law as a direct source of obligations.

Official sources used for mapping:
- https://law.qu.edu.qa/en-us/Colleges/law/departments/private-law/Pages/course-description.aspx
- Qatar University undergraduate catalog / College of Law study plan.

## 4. Qatari Civil Code legal-source boundary
Primary statutory source:
**Law No. 22 of 2004 Promulgating the Civil Code**, Al Meezan legal portal.

Main source-of-obligation structure used by LexLearn:
- Contract — starts at Article 64.
- Unilateral will — Articles 192 onward; Article 193 includes promise of reward.
- Unlawful act / tort — Article 199 and following.
- Unjustified enrichment — Article 220 and following.
- Law as a direct source — Article 240.

Primary portal:
- https://www.almeezan.qa/LawView.aspx?LawID=2559&language=ar&opt=

## 5. LexLearn six-week pilot coverage
The production pilot keeps one subject complete rather than spreading thinly across many subjects.

| Week | Academic coverage | Adaptive emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contract formation, consent, object/cause, special form, validity | Recall → understanding → legal precision |
| 2 | Expression of will, offer/acceptance, capacity and defects of consent | Precision + application |
| 3 | Effects of contract, performance and contractual liability | Source/effect distinction + application |
| 4 | Unilateral will, promise of reward, law as direct source | Classification + transfer |
| 5 | Tort (personal/others/things), unjust enrichment, undue payment, officious management | Issue spotting + application |
| 6 | Integrated source classification and exam simulation | Legal precision + exam execution + retention |

## 6. Content governance rule
Curriculum mapping does **not** auto-approve questions. Production content follows:
`draft → legal_review → learning_review → approved → retired`.

Only `approved` content may be used by grounded semantic AI grading. Official curriculum pages establish what should be taught; statutory/legal sources establish the legal rule used for grading.
