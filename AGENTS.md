# LexLearn learner experience

- Write every learner-facing free-response question and feedback message for a first-year law student with no prior legal education. Use short sentences, one action per step, and explain procedural terms such as issue, rule and application in everyday words.
- Preserve all legally decisive facts, qualifications and requested rubric elements when simplifying a question. Do not reveal the answer through hints or add new requirements. Keep original approved prompts available for checking equivalence.
- Review Arabic and English presentation whenever changing learner questions. Test all affected bank items, including compound questions and review sessions.
- Display only saved, server-confirmed completion and supported scores. Celebrate effort/completion without claiming legal mastery or a passing academic grade.
- Keep celebration brief, silent, dismiss-free and compatible with reduced-motion preferences. Never replay it automatically on report reload or use celebration state to authorize progress.


- Identify each legal provision with its jurisdiction, law name, number and year. Keep this context visible during questions, feedback and results, even when statutory texts are hidden. Never infer a law from an article number alone.
