# Complete statutory identification

The approved Qatar Theory of Rights course now identifies the Qatar Civil Code, promulgated by Law No. 22 of 2004, on every statutory caption, question, saved-answer review and report. The downloadable result card includes this law identity.

Approved question records and rubrics remain unchanged. A display formatter expands known course references; unknown article numbers are not assigned a law. The legal context remains visible when the original statutory text is hidden for assessment.

Validation: 25 article captions, bilingual diagnostic and all 30 sessions, 72 bilingual training prompts, case-guide parsing, result-card export, and the existing publication test suite.
