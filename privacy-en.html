Privacy Policy — LexLearn

Version: 17 October 2026 — 2026-10-17-v5

Information we process

Basic account and study information, registered courses, student answers, assessment results, response times, learning progress and educational review records. We avoid collecting personal information unnecessary for learning.

Why we process it

To provide adaptive training, assess progress, enable authorized supervisors to help students and protect the service.

Registration and approval

For the conference period, students can create an account and sign in immediately with a generated username and a password of their choice, without supervisor approval or contact verification. Email and WhatsApp are both optional contact details and are not verified identities. Without verified contact information, self-service password recovery might not be possible. Supervisors can suspend or disable accounts under the published rules.

Assessment technology

Some features use limited local rule-based checks; other AI-assisted evaluation functions may be enabled separately. Where evidence is insufficient, the service should abstain from assigning a reliable score and allow supervisor review. Educational indications are not official university grades.

Who sees the information?

The student may access their records when active. Authorized staff can access student records within their approved permissions. Passwords are not disclosed to supervisors as readable records.

Exports and deletion

Authorized staff may create printable reports, PDFs and copyable text containing student records; these documents must be stored and shared securely. Accounts may be paused or disabled while preserving progress. Permanent deletion may irreversibly remove related answers and evaluations, subject to applicable retention requirements; limited administrative audit records may remain.

Retention

Institutional partners will determine specific retention periods before a formal university pilot, subject to the applicable requirements.

Conference contact options

The LexLearn Web Summit Qatar 2027 conference page offers an optional contact form. It collects your name, role (student, university or investor/partner), preferred contact details (email or WhatsApp), and optionally your organisation. We use these details only to respond to your inquiry and follow up about relevant LexLearn academic pilots or partnerships. Secure storage requires a separate, unticked consent box and a successful security check; the page confirms receipt only after storage succeeds. If secure intake has not been enabled, visitors may instead contact us directly by WhatsApp or email, and their form details are not stored in the conference inquiry database.

Inquiries are processed in Supabase with server-only permissions and cannot be read by other visitors. Limited technical information, including a keyed pseudonymous network fingerprint, is used to prevent abuse; Cloudflare Turnstile checks are used when secure intake is active. Our intended retention limit for inactive inquiries is 12 months, subject to an operational review and deletion process and applicable legal requirements; limited consent or compliance evidence may be retained when necessary. Direct WhatsApp and email communications are also subject to the chosen provider's policies. Proposed university pilot reporting will be aggregated and anonymised, subject to required approvals.
