01 / THE PROBLEM

The same score can hide different learning problems.

One student remembers a rule but struggles to explain it. Another understands the idea but cannot recall it during an exam. A single score does not reveal the next useful step.

THE LEXLEARN RESPONSE

Identify the gap. Build the weaker skill from the stronger one.

Diagnosis guides targeted practice while students remain independent and academic supervisors keep control.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Five precise skills. One adaptable path.

The five-axis framework is the product vision; the public demo currently illustrates recall and understanding only.

01

Recall

Retrieve the legal rule accurately.

02

Understanding

Explain why the legal rule works.

03

Application

Apply the rule to a new set of facts.

04

Legal precision

Use accurate legal terms and conditions.

05

Exam answer structure

Build a clear, supported legal answer.

Example of the adaptive principle

Strong recall + weaker understanding → practise explaining each element of the rule. Strong understanding + weaker recall → create memory cues from the concept already understood.

05 / UNIVERSITY PILOT

Measurable academic collaboration

We propose a focused, faculty-supervised academic pilot to measure learning impact before expanding.

30–60students proposedProposed number of law students participating in the pilot.
6–8weeks proposedProposed duration for training and before/after assessment.

01BeforeEstablish a baseline using skill-specific tasks.

02DuringDeliver structured practice under faculty supervision.

03AfterReview usability, scoring agreement and early indicators.

What we need from the university

An academic supervisor, a student group and a room and time for the sessions.

What the university receives

A learning-impact measurement report and a results report.

Student results will be de-identified and shared only in aggregated reports, subject to academic consent and safeguards.
