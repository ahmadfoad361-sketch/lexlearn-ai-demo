Terms of Use — LexLearn

Educational purpose

LexLearn is an adaptive legal-learning prototype designed to support diagnosis, practice, revision and legal answer construction. Content is not professional legal advice.

Account and access

Students should keep their sign-in credentials private. New conference accounts can be used immediately without contact verification or supervisor approval. Students must retain their issued username and password; optional contact details are not verified. The authorized institution may pause, disable or remove an account in accordance with its policies and privacy requirements.

Legal and academic content

Academic content must undergo relevant legal and pedagogical review before being declared approved. Source versions and assessment evidence should be retained where available.

Automated assessment

Automated feedback may be provisional. Where legal meaning is uncertain or conflicting, supervisor review is required. Results are not official university marks unless expressly adopted by the institution.

Institutional pilots

Specific academic, privacy and processing agreements may apply before any formal pilot begins.
